About IronHoof

CRAFTING
EXCELLENCE
SINCE 2015

IronHoof Wheels is a premier manufacturer of high-performance automotive wheels, headquartered in Chengdu, China. We specialize in flow-formed, forged monoblock, and heavy-duty off-road wheels for the global market.

Our state-of-the-art facility combines CNC precision machining with rigorous quality control, ensuring every wheel meets or exceeds JWL, VIA, and TUV standards. From concept to completion, we deliver wheels that balance aggressive aesthetics with uncompromising structural integrity.

Trusted by distributors across North America, Europe, Australia, and Southeast Asia, IronHoof has become the go-to choice for enthusiasts who refuse to compromise.
